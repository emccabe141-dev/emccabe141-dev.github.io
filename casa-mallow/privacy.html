Casa Mallow privacy policy

Made by Mallow. Last updated 5 November 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Casa Mallow has no accounts and no servers. Everything you add (tasks, routines, notes, names, photos) is stored only on your own phone.

What leaves your phone

Nothing you add is sent to us or to anyone else. The app’s fonts and icons are built in, so it works offline and makes no outside requests.

Backups

If you use the backup button, the app saves a file to your phone’s downloads. Where that file goes next is up to you.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone’s settings, deletes everything.

Children

Casa Mallow is for parents and carers. It is not designed for children to use on their own.
